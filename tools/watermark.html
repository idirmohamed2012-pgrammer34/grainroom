Add a watermark to photos

Protect your work with a custom text watermark. Place it in a corner, in the centre or tile it diagonally across the image.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ Batch

How to use it

  1. Drop your photos on the box above.
  2. Type your watermark and set position, size, opacity and colour.
  3. Download the watermarked images or a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Can I watermark many photos at once?

Yes, every photo you add gets the same watermark and updates live when you change an option.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The watermark is drawn on your device.

What makes a good watermark?

A semi-transparent name or handle with a soft shadow stays readable on light and dark areas without ruining the photo. Tiled mode is harder to crop out.
