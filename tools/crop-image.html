Crop images to social media sizes

Get the exact pixel size each platform wants. Move the focus point to choose which part of the photo stays in frame.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ Batch

How to use it

  1. Drop your images on the box above.
  2. Pick a preset like Instagram post or YouTube thumbnail, or enter a custom size.
  3. Adjust the horizontal and vertical focus, then download.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

How does the crop work?

The image is scaled to fill the target size and the extra edges are cut off. The focus sliders decide which side is trimmed.

Which sizes are available?

Instagram post, portrait and story, YouTube thumbnail, Facebook post and cover, X header, LinkedIn banner, a square ID size and custom.
