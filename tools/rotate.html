Rotate and flip images online

Fix sideways photos or mirror an image. Works on many files at once.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ Batch

How to use it

  1. Drop your images on the box above.
  2. Choose rotate 90° left or right, 180°, or flip horizontally or vertically.
  3. Download the results or a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does rotating lose quality?

Rotating by 90° steps is exact. Saving as JPG or WebP re-encodes the image, so use quality 92% or higher, or choose PNG for no loss.

Can I rotate many photos?

Yes. Every file you add gets the same action.
