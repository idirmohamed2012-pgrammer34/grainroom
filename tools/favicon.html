Favicon generator

Create every icon size your website needs from one image. Download a ZIP with favicon.ico, 16/32/48 px PNGs, the Apple touch icon and 192/512 px Android icons.

✓ Free✓ No upload

How to use it

  1. Drop your logo or image (a square one works best).
  2. Optionally set a background colour and padding.
  3. Download the ZIP and copy the HTML lines from the included text file into your site head.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

What sizes are included?

favicon.ico (16, 32, 48), PNGs at 16, 32 and 48, apple-touch-icon at 180, and Android icons at 192 and 512.

What image should I start with?

A square image of at least 512 × 512 px. Simple logos with a transparent background work best.
