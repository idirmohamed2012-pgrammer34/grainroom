Photo collage maker

Combine several photos into a clean grid. Adjust columns, cell shape, spacing and corners, then download a high-resolution image.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ No watermark

How to use it

  1. Add two or more photos.
  2. Choose columns, cell shape, spacing, corner radius and background colour.
  3. Download the collage as JPG or PNG.

Frequently asked questions

How many photos can I add?

As many as you like. Large collages may take a moment on older devices.

What resolution is the result?

Choose 1200, 2000 or 3000 px wide.

Can I make more complex layouts?

Yes, open the Grainroom editor to arrange layers freely with masks, text and filters.
