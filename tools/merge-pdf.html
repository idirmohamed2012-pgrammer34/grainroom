Merge PDF files online

Join PDFs into a single file. Reorder them with the arrows, then download the merged document.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ No watermark

How to use it

  1. Add two or more PDF files.
  2. Reorder them with the arrows.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and download.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Is there a size or page limit?

No fixed limit. Very large files depend on your device memory.

Does merging change the quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and images keep their quality.

Can it merge password-protected PDFs?

Not if they need a password to open. Remove the password first.
