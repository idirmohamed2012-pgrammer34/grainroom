Remove EXIF data and GPS location from photos

Photos often carry hidden details: where they were taken, when, and with which device. Strip them in one click before posting online.

How to use it

  1. Drop your photos on the box above.

  3. Download the clean copies or a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

What is EXIF data?

EXIF is metadata stored inside a photo: camera make and model, date and time, exposure settings and often the GPS coordinates where it was taken.

Is JPG quality affected?

No. For JPG the metadata blocks are cut out without re-encoding, so pixels are untouched. Colour profiles are kept so colours stay correct.

What about the rotation of my photo?

If a photo relies on an EXIF orientation flag, it is rotated into the correct position and re-saved at high quality so it stays upright after cleaning.

Does it work for PNG and WebP?

Yes. They are re-encoded, which drops embedded metadata.

Does it upload my photos?

Never. This matters most for a privacy tool: all processing happens on your device.
