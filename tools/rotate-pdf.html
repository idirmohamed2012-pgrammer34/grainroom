Rotate PDF pages online

Fix scanned documents that came out sideways. Rotate every page or only the ones you choose.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ No watermark

How to use it

  1. Add a PDF.
  2. Choose the rotation and, optionally, which pages.
  3. Click Rotate PDF and download.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes, enter ranges like 2-4, 7. Leave blank for all pages.

Is quality kept?

Yes. The rotation is stored in the PDF and nothing is recompressed.
