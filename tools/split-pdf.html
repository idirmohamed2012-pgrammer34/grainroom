Split PDF and extract pages

Pull out the pages you need, split into parts, or save every page as its own PDF.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ No watermark

How to use it

  1. Add a PDF. The page count appears.
  2. Type pages or ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8-.
  3. Choose one PDF with those pages, one PDF per range, or every page separately, then download.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

What is the range format?

Use commas and dashes: 1-3, 5, 8- means pages 1 to 3, page 5 and page 8 to the end.

Is quality kept?

Yes. Pages are copied without recompressing.
