Resize images online

Change the pixel size of many images at once. High-quality downscaling keeps edges sharp.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ Batch

How to use it

  1. Drop your images on the box above.
  2. Choose how to resize: width, height, percent, fit in a box, or exact size.
  3. Pick the output format and download the images or a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Does resizing reduce quality?

Shrinking uses multi-step high-quality scaling, so results stay sharp. Enlarging cannot add real detail, which is why "Never enlarge" is on by default.

Can I keep the aspect ratio?

Yes. Width, height, percent and fit modes always keep the ratio. Only "Exact size" stretches.

What size should I use for the web?

For full-width web photos 1600–2000 px wide is plenty. For social posts 1080 px is typical.
