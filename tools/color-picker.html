Image color picker and palette extractor

Pick exact colours from any image and get the dominant palette in one go. Click a swatch to copy its HEX code.

✓ Free✓ No upload

How to use it

  1. Add an image.
  2. Move the cursor over it to read colours and click to save a swatch.
  3. Click any swatch to copy its HEX code. The dominant palette is shown automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Which colour formats are shown?

HEX, RGB and HSL.

How is the palette computed?

Pixels are grouped into similar colours and the most common distinct groups are shown.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Everything stays on your device.
