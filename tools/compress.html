How to use it

  1. Drop your images on the box above.
  2. Pick WebP for the smallest files or JPG for maximum compatibility, then set the quality (75% is a good start).
  3. Optionally limit the width, then download the results or the ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I reduce the size?

Photos typically shrink by 60–90% at 70–80% quality with little visible difference. Screenshots and graphics with flat colours compress even better as WebP.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser using the built-in image encoder.

JPG or WebP?

WebP is usually 25–35% smaller at the same quality and is supported by all modern browsers. Use JPG when you need to share with older software.

Does it keep transparency?

WebP keeps transparency. JPG cannot, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour you choose.
