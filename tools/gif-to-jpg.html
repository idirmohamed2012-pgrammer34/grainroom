GIF to JPG converter

Convert GIF files to JPG in one click, right in your browser. Extracts the first frame of the GIF as a JPG.

✓ Free✓ No upload✓ Batch

How to use it

  1. Drop one or many GIF files onto the box above (or paste from the clipboard).
  2. Adjust quality or background colour if you want.
  3. Download each JPG file or grab them all in a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Is this GIF to JPG converter really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no watermark and no limit on the number of files, because the conversion runs on your own device.

Are my GIF files uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are decoded and re-encoded locally by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, so it also works offline once the page has loaded.

Will converting GIF to JPG change the quality?

JPG is lossy but at 90% quality the difference is invisible. For animated GIFs only the first frame is converted.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Drop as many as you like. Each one is converted separately and you can download them together as a ZIP.
