About Grainroom App

A free photo editor and image toolkit that lives in your browser.

What it is

Grainroom App is a web app: open the page and start editing. There is nothing to install and no account to create. The editor offers layers, masks, selections, free transform, retouching, film looks, text and stickers. The tools handle quick jobs such as compressing, converting and resizing.

Web app vs. mobile app

Grainroom App (this site, grainroomapp.vercel.app) runs in your browser on any device with a modern browser. Grainroom Photo Studio is a separate mobile app. They are different products with different features.

How it works

Everything is processed by your browser using the canvas and standard web APIs. Your photos are not uploaded to a server. After the first visit the editor and tools are cached so they keep working offline. Details are on the privacy page.
