Frequently asked questions

Is Grainroom App the same as the Grainroom Photo Studio mobile app?

No. Grainroom App is the web app you use online, in your browser, with no download. Grainroom Photo Studio is a separate mobile app. Your web edits stay in the browser on your device.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Grainroom App runs entirely in your web browser (sans téléchargement). You can optionally install it as an app from your browser menu to use it offline.

Is Grainroom really free?

Yes. Completely free, no account required, no watermarks on exports.

Does my photo leave my computer?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Can I use it for commercial work?

Yes. You can use the editor and the files you export for personal and commercial projects.

Is this affiliated with Adobe?

No. Grainroom is an independent free alternative. “Photoshop” is a trademark of Adobe Inc.
